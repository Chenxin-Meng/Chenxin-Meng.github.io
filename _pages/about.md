I am a student in the 2025 Mathematics Elite Class at East China Normal University, and I graduated from Yunyang High School in Shiyan, Hubei Province.
My research interests center on algebraic number theory, particularly class field theory and its arithmetic applications. Currently, under my mentor's guidance, I am studying modular forms in preparation for entering the research frontier of algebraic number theory. I look forward to finding my own problems at the intersection of number theory and arithmetic geometry.
